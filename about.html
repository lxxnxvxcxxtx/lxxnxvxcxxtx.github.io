about me

MY WORK IS ABOUT USING VIDEO AS A CREATIVE TOOL; FOR PLAY, FOR STORYTELLING, & FOR HELPING OTHERS SHARE THEIR OWN WORK IN A WAY THAT FEELS TRUE TO THEM

who am i?

i'm a filmmaker & producer who enjoys making work that's playful, visually engaging, &, most importantly, honest.

i collaborate with musicians, artists, designers, & brands to turn their ideas into videos that feel authentic to them.

whether it's an experimental short or a client project, my goal is always the same:
to make something that people enjoy watching & that reflects the personality of the creatives behind it.
